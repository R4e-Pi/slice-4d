A parcel is ground only where it has been inscribed. A lit edge has land beyond it: step off and that parcel appears. A bare edge has nothing past it, so rotate and walk along the chain, where other blocks hold different land.

  • Left thumb walk, any direction
  • Rotate 4D turn into the chain
  • drag elsewhere to orbit
  • WASD walk
  • Space jump
  • E rotate into the chain
  • M map
  • R restart
  • drag to orbit

This game needs WebGL, which isn't available in this browser.
